BETTING ON ADA: How Ada's Automation Elevated Betsson Group's Award-Winning CX

================================================================================

COMPANY OVERVIEW

As a global leader in customer experience in the gaming industry, Betsson Group
has been recognized as the Customer Service Operator of the Year for four
consecutive years at the EGR Operator Awards. With more than five decades of
experience and 600,000 active customers, Betsson Group is one of the world's
largest gaming organizations, with plans to further this growth.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

BETSSON GROUP'S CX CHALLENGE

Motivated by a vision 'to provide the best customer experience in the industry',
Betsson Group knew it had to stay ahead of customer expectations by introducing
an automated self-service solution. To better meet its global customers' needs,
the business wanted a solution that would extend support hours to 24/7 while
introducing personalized support available in every customer language.

Customers needed the ability to:

- Answer questions specific to deposit and withdrawal issues and concerns
- Receive targeted promotions based on search behavior
- Learn about Betsson Group's offerings and compare against competitors

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE SOLUTION

With a goal to strengthen its award-winning customer experience through digital
transformation, Betsson Group chose Ada to scale automated support across its
top two websites, Betsson and Betsafe.

In less than a month, Betsson Group's customer service team used Ada's
AI-powered platform to build and launch two chatbots ready to provide customers
with 24/7 multilingual assistance.

Connecting Ada with the company's back-end allows customers to use the chatbot
to handle deposit and withdrawal questions, receive promotions, and learn about
offerings.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

KEY METRICS AND RESULTS

Before Ada:
- 100% of all inquiries handled by agents
- Agents overwhelmed during big games and peak seasons

After Ada:
- 45% of all chatbot inquiries resolved by Ada
- Agents freed for high-value customer inquiries
- 90%+ CSAT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE ADA DIFFERENCE

Introducing AI-powered CX in Weeks

Guided by Ada's ACX Consultants, members of Betsson Group's customer service
team designed and launched two AI-powered chatbots in under a month. With
minimal dependence on IT, the team designed the automated experience with
engaging multimedia content, including videos, images, GIFs and links.

Learning from the Customer Journey

With thousands of inquiries automated each month, Betsson Group gains new data
and insights that they can use to strengthen their chatbots' performance while
improving platforms, offerings, and processes across their business.

Enabling a Streamlined AI to Agent Handoff

By connecting Ada with Betsson Group's existing live chat vendor, when a
customer requests an agent, they're provided with a link that seamlessly
escalates them from chatbot to live chat. To accelerate time to resolution, the
agent is provided with the chatbot transcript so that they can pick up the
conversation where the chatbot left off.

Maintaining an Industry-Leading CX Reputation

Despite initial fears that a chatbot may hinder the customer experience, the
strength of Ada's automation has enabled Betsson Group to maintain its CSAT and
industry-leading CX leadership, winning the '2019 Customer Service Operator of
the Year' award by EGR (eGaming Review), for the fourth time in a row.

Reaching International Audiences

Leveraging Ada's Multilingual feature, Betsson Group creates and automates
content in English, then instantly translates it to six languages, including:
German, Finnish, Swedish, Norwegian, Danish and Spanish. This allows customers
around the world to access automated self-service in the language of their
choice.

Launching Ada Across New Brands

With thousands of inquiries automated each month, Betsson Group gains new data
and insights that they can use to strengthen their chatbots' performance while
improving platforms, offerings, and processes across their business.
