CEBU PACIFIC: REDEFINING TRAVEL CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE WITH ADA'S AI AGENTS

About Cebu Pacific
-------------------
Cebu Pacific is the Philippines' largest airline by passenger volume, serving
over 20 million passengers annually across 14 countries. The airline is focused
on making travel accessible for all customers and is positioned as a leader in
customer experience innovation in Southeast Asia.


The Challenge
-------------
Cebu Pacific needed to support high volumes of travelers quickly while
maintaining consistency. The risk of long wait times, uneven answers, and
frustrated passengers during stressful travel moments was a persistent
challenge. Their initial scripted chatbot, named Charlie, offered basic
responses that lacked personalization, and maintaining it required heavy manual
effort. Cebu Pacific needed a smarter, more scalable solution.


The Solution: Partnership with Ada
-----------------------------------
Cebu Pacific partnered with Ada, a global leader in AI customer service, to
transform Charlie from a scripted chatbot into a generative AI agent -- smarter,
faster, and able to deliver more human-like support at scale. This made Cebu
Pacific the first airline in Southeast Asia to fully integrate generative AI
across its customer support channels.

Charlie is now positioned as the first point of contact across the channels
customers use most, instead of routing customers through clunky menus or making
them wait in queues. The AI agent is deployed across Cebu Pacific's mobile app,
Facebook Messenger, and the Cebu Pacific website, offering consistent support
in multiple languages including English and Tagalog.

With the move to generative AI, Charlie now pulls answers directly from Cebu
Pacific's robust, real-time, centralized knowledge base, eliminating the need
to hardcode flows or upload content manually. The AI agent delivers contextual
responses, can link customers to relevant resources, and handles inquiries
end-to-end whenever possible.

Initial capabilities include handling common passenger inquiries such as flight
bookings, itinerary changes, and travel documentation requirements, with
around-the-clock real-time assistance. Features include personalized and
contextually relevant interactions, seamless integration with existing Cebu
Pacific systems, and accurate, timely updates on bookings and flight statuses.


Key Metrics and Results
-----------------------
- 75% of customer interactions resolved autonomously
- 50% increase in customer satisfaction (CSAT) scores
- 34% increase in automated resolution rate
- Less than 1-minute wait times for high-priority cases
- Single-digit minute wait times for access to live agents on second-priority
  issues
- 24/7 real-time customer support across 11 languages


Organizational Transformation
-----------------------------
Ada gave Cebu Pacific an operating model that taught them that it is not only
the technology that will change, but also the organization and how they set
themselves up to support the new technology.

Customer Care teams hold daily stand-ups to review escalations, analyze
intents, and fine-tune how the AI agent handles edge cases. The customer care
and CX teams also meet weekly to collaborate on open items and review recent
updates. When urgent issues arise, they immediately coach the AI agent or
refine knowledge base articles, ensuring the team is always agile and the AI
agent is always up to date.

Future Direction
----------------
The next phase of Cebu Pacific's AI customer service strategy focuses on
expanding to new channels, starting with email. The customer care team is
actively working on applying Ada's AI agent to automate and accelerate email
support. Other planned expansions include proactive notifications, expanded
multilingual support, and personalized travel recommendations.
