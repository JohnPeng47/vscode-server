CLICKUP ELEVATES THEIR CX EXPERIENCE WITH ADA'S AI AGENT

================================================================================

ABOUT CLICKUP
-------------
ClickUp is an all-in-one project management tool that integrates with platforms
across a company's tech stack to consolidate workflows. As of the time of this
case study, ClickUp's customer base had grown to 12 million users.

THE CHALLENGE
-------------
ClickUp's customer base exploded to 12 million users, but their support
couldn't keep up. Legacy email forms and basic chat slowed down resolution and
left agents scrambling for context. The support team operated as generalists --
each agent was a "jack-of-all-trades" with limited knowledge about a broad
range of products and topics, rather than having deep expertise in any single
area.

THE SOLUTION
------------
ClickUp partnered with Ada to deploy an AI-powered customer service agent. By
integrating Ada's AI agent with their Zendesk Help Center, ClickUp enables the
AI agent to synthesize support content with real-time user data, delivering
more accurate, personalized answers.

ClickUp uses its generative AI chatbot to give customers access to instant
product information and a seamless handoff to the right specialist when
necessary. With the AI agent handling routine customer inquiries automatically,
human support agents were freed to shift into specialized roles.

THE RESULTS
-----------
Ada's AI Agent enabled ClickUp to achieve performance comparable to experienced
human agents while introducing innovative problem-solving approaches, resulting
in enhanced productivity and collaboration capabilities.

Key outcomes:

- AI manages routine customer inquiries automatically, eliminating the need for
  human intervention to address new customer inquiries that come through when
  the product is updated.

- Human support agents shifted from generalists to specialized roles focused
  on:
    - Product adoption
    - Churn reduction
    - Collaboration with product teams
    - Enhanced customer experience delivery
    - Deepening product expertise
    - Driving strategic initiatives

- ClickUp adopted Automated Resolution Rate as a core KPI for their digital
  support team, reported up to senior leadership because of the efficiencies
  that Ada is driving through the business.

- The AI Agent's seamless integration set new industry benchmarks for CX
  excellence and served as a significant growth driver for ClickUp.

- The support function scaled alongside business expansion without
  proportional headcount increases.
