How Digicel Repositioned as a Digital Operator with Ada's Conversational AI

ABOUT DIGICEL

Digicel is a leader in mobile phone networks and home entertainment for the
Caribbean, Latin America, and South Pacific regions. The company had a mission
to reposition itself as a true digital operator. To build a compelling digital
service model, they knew they needed to gain efficiencies outside of their
legacy CX systems and call centers.

THE CHALLENGE

90%+ of Digicel's support interactions were received via phone. At scale, this
model could not support their efficiency goals. As the Caribbean's largest
telco, Digicel had a responsibility to their customers to deliver the
best-in-class communication experiences they demanded.

Digicel needed a digital tool that would support the 5 local languages and the
nuanced local requirements of the 30+ regional markets they were serving.

THE SOLUTION

After evaluating different vendors, Neil O'Hanlon, Director of CX at Digicel,
and his team chose to partner with Ada to automate and scale their customer
experience with industry-leading conversational AI.

With Ada's implementation team, Digicel was able to meet all of their language
and regional requirements by launching a single CX chatbot named Ruby. The
solution is conversational in 5 languages their customers needed: Haitian
Creole, Spanish, French, Dutch, and English.

With Ada, Digicel could distinguish between 32 regions to ensure customers
received relevant content such as pricing, plans, and top-ups.

Digicel promoted their customer service options, actually encouraging valuable
customer conversations with their CX chatbot, Ruby -- achieving contextual and
personalized customer experiences that run 24/7.

QUOTE

Neil O'Hanlon, Director of CX at Digicel:

  "We now have our single bot fronting 50 different departments across 32
   different markets -- all with an instantaneous handover to a live agent."

KEY METRICS AND RESULTS

  - 135k engaged conversations per month
  - $750k saved in service costs (2020)
  - Increased chat volume without increasing headcount
  - Live chat agents are more responsive than ever before -- 90% of chats
    responded to in less than 10 seconds
  - Consistent positive tNPS scores
  - Strong containment rates
  - Repeat usage from happy customers

IMPACT

The high recognition and containment rates seen with Ada's conversational AI
meant Digicel could scale confidently and move away from reliance on costly
phone-first support operations. By implementing conversational AI on their
website, Digicel was able to divert 135k conversations per month away from
call centers, saving $750k in service costs.

Digicel successfully shifted from a phone-heavy support model (90%+ phone) to
a balanced omnichannel approach, with their Ruby chatbot serving as a central
component of their digital transformation across multiple languages and
Caribbean, Latin American, and South Pacific markets.

IMPLEMENTATION DETAILS

  - Single bot serving 32 markets
  - 5 languages: Haitian Creole, Spanish, French, Dutch, English
  - 50 departments fronted by the bot
  - Instantaneous handover to live agents
  - 24/7 availability
  - Region-specific content (pricing, plans, top-ups)
