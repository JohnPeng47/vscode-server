================================================================================

EPOS NOW CASE STUDY
How Epos Now Saved 60,000+ Hours Monthly with Ada's AI Agent

================================================================================

ABOUT EPOS NOW

Epos Now is a leading AI-driven point-of-sale (POS) and embedded finance platform.
The company operates in 10 countries, including the US, and supports 80,000+ small
and medium-sized business (SMB) locations. Epos Now empowers underserved merchants
in retail and hospitality globally with cloud-based POS systems.

================================================================================

KEY METRICS

- 60,000+ human labor hours saved per month
- Up to 70% of support demand automated
- 30% increase in CSAT (customer satisfaction) on messaging channels
- 22-25% drop in overall call demand
- 15% increase in CSAT on the voice channel (within weeks of voice launch)
- 40% of all phone inquiries automatically resolved
- 65% average automated resolution rate across channels

================================================================================

THE CHALLENGE

Epos Now's customers often need guidance to identify which features of the platform
will be most beneficial to their unique business operations. Customers were asking
for detailed, multi-step guidance to run their businesses efficiently. They
frequently bypassed existing long-form support documentation, preferring human
interaction to navigate complex challenges instead.

The primary challenges included managing a complex product with numerous features,
serving a diverse customer base across multiple industries, and providing efficient
support for business-critical functions like payment processing. Epos Now needed a
solution that could offer personalized, instantaneous support while managing these
complexities. As they scaled, the support challenges grew increasingly complex.

================================================================================

THE SOLUTION: SIDEKICK

In August 2023, Epos Now took a bold step by implementing an AI agent to transform
their customer service operations. They partnered with Ada to launch Sidekick, their
AI agent, on messaging channels to handle complex inquiries in real time, delivering
instant, seamless support.

Sidekick leverages machine learning and Natural Language Processing (NLP) to provide
24/7 customer support across Epos Now's website, mobile app, and point-of-sale
systems through a conversational interface. The platform delivers quick, precise, and
mindful responses to even the most complex inquiries while significantly reducing
wait times.

Epos Now later expanded Sidekick to voice support, where it now automatically
resolves 40% of all phone inquiries.

================================================================================

IMPLEMENTATION APPROACH

Epos Now conducted thorough testing before full implementation, running A/B tests
comparing the AI agent's performance to that of human agents on real customer
interactions.

Instead of hiring additional resources or bringing in external AI experts, Epos Now
elevated existing employees who were already adept at delivering excellent customer
service. They identified team members who excelled at delivering great customer
experiences and tasked them with training and managing the AI agent. Over time, the
team specialized in monitoring AI conversations, refining customer workflows, and
ensuring that Sidekick was continuously aligned with product updates.

This shift allowed Epos Now to better serve customers while empowering their staff to
take on higher-level responsibilities.

================================================================================

RESULTS

Since its launch, Sidekick has delivered transformative results:

Sidekick saved over 60,000 human labor hours each month in the year since launch,
automated up to 70% of support demand, increased CSAT by 30%, and streamlined
support processes across the organization.

Within a few weeks of launch, customer satisfaction (CSAT) on chat interactions rose
by 30%, and overall call demand dropped by 22% to 25% as more customers shifted to
channels automated by their AI agent.

With a 60% to 70% average automated resolution rate, Epos Now dramatically reduced
the need for human intervention in routine queries.

On the voice channel, within weeks of launching, Epos Now saw a 15% increase in
CSAT, and Sidekick now automatically resolves 40% of all phone inquiries.

Even when Sidekick cannot fully resolve a customer inquiry, it still reduces the
overall effort required by human agents. Thanks to Sidekick's ability to understand
the query from the start, much of the behind-the-scenes work -- like creating a
case, documenting the interaction, and taking notes -- has already been done by the
time the customer is handed off. When the inquiry reaches a human agent, everything
is already captured and organized, leaving them to simply update the case with the
resolution.
