IPSY: Transforming Customer Experience with Ada's AI Platform

About IPSY

IPSY is one of the world's largest personalized beauty subscription companies,
serving over 3.5 million subscribers. The company was formed through the merger
of two leading beauty subscription brands -- IPSY and BoxyCharm -- which came
together in late 2020. IPSY delivers highly personalized beauty experiences
using advanced product innovation and machine learning technology.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Challenge

When IPSY and BoxyCharm merged, they were using different AI support solutions
to manage product, subscription, and delivery inquiries. The fragmented
approach created inconsistencies in customer experience across the two brands.

Prior to implementing Ada, BoxyCharm's customer service metrics were:

  - Average first response time: ~29 hours
  - Total average resolution time: 53 hours
  - Customer satisfaction (CSAT) score: 58%

IPSY members often had to wait multiple hours for a response, and the existing
conversational AI solution was not delivering the results the team needed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Solution

BoxyCharm was the first to adopt Ada, launching their conversational AI bot
("Boxybot") in June 2020. The focus was on providing fast and effective answers
to FAQs, order inquiries, and a smooth handoff to live agent support. Ada's
automation capabilities served customers' in-the-moment needs and enabled
agents to do more impactful work with contextual handoffs from Boxybot. Ada
also helped capture important details from customers before passing them to
agents, reducing the volume of interactions that get handed off and enabling
agents to resolve issues faster.

Inspired by BoxyCharm's success with Ada and motivated to replicate their
results, the IPSY team decided to replace their existing conversational AI
solution with Ada. On May 26, IPSY launched "Glam Bot," integrating existing
content from Kustomer to Ada and Kustomer ticketing on the IPSY Help Centre.

The Glam Bot was launched in a record 12 days.

Quote: "We launched [Glam Bot] in a record 12 days! [We] couldn't have asked
for a better implementation and post-implementation process."

IPSY now has fully-enabled bot management resources to manage both BoxyCharm's
Boxybot and IPSY's Glam Bot in partnership with cross-functional stakeholders
within the Care team. IPSY uses Ada to automate their top drivers for brand
interactions that don't require agent support, freeing agents to focus on more
complex use cases and ensuring their time is best utilized.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Key Results

Combining visionary leadership with Ada's ACX (AI Customer Experience)
platform, IPSY achieved significant improvements across all key metrics:

  - CSAT score increased from 58% to 76% (a 41% improvement)
  - Average first response time reduced from ~29 hours to less than 2 hours
  - Total average resolution time reduced from 53 hours to 19 hours
  - Both CSAT and Automated Resolution rate increased
  - 943% ROI on their generative AI investment
  - Led the company's first successful AI implementation

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

What's Next

The potential to extend the value of their conversational AI is top of mind
for Skidmore (VP of Customer Care). IPSY is in the process of building out
retention workflows to support their subscription models and drive growth.

Upcoming initiatives with Ada include:

  - Implementing a new system to authenticate conversations and provide order
    details and statuses for monthly subscriptions
  - Instagram channel integration
  - Custom validation on tracking numbers
  - Expanding to additional social channels including Facebook and WhatsApp

With Ada, IPSY can offer a more personalized approach and consistent brand
presence across all brand interactions, with the opportunity to expand to
social channels and continue scaling their automation-first strategy.
