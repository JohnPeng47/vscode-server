Case Study: Life360

================================================================================

Life360 Increases Their Support Capacity 5x with Ada's AI Agent

================================================================================

COMPANY OVERVIEW

Life360 is a location-sharing and family safety platform serving over 80 million
users. As their user base expanded significantly, customer support demand
increased proportionally, requiring a shift from manual, email-heavy operations
to a more scalable, AI-first approach.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE CHALLENGE

With millions of families relying on Life360's safety and location-sharing
features, the demand for customer support was surging. The company needed a way
to scale efficiently while ensuring fast, high-quality service.

Before implementing Ada, Life360's support model was heavily reliant on email,
with over 90% of inquiries handled through that channel. This led to long
response times, increasing operational strain, and a growing backlog of
unresolved tickets. Email was Life360's largest channel, carrying over 90% of
their customer support volume, which often resulted in long wait times and
delayed resolutions.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE SOLUTION

Life360 partnered with Ada to launch Vida, an AI agent designed to automate
routine inquiries, enhance efficiency, and deliver a more seamless customer
experience. The deployment transitioned Life360 to an AI-first support approach,
transforming customer interactions from transactional to conversational.

Life360 trained Vida to recognize high-touch scenarios requiring human expertise.
Sensitive cases -- such as crash detection scenarios -- are seamlessly escalated
to human agents for empathetic handling, ensuring critical customer interactions
receive appropriate personal attention.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

KEY METRICS AND RESULTS

- 5x increase in overall customer support capacity
- 70% automated resolution rate achieved immediately upon deployment
- 400 FTE workload equivalent handled by Vida (the AI agent)
- 3x faster response times compared to human agents
- 150,000+ inquiries managed monthly by Vida
- 30,000 inquiries handled monthly by human agents across all channels
- 80 million users supported

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

IMPACT AND OUTCOMES

Today, 70% of inquiries are resolved without human intervention, freeing up
agents to focus on more complex, high-value cases, while ensuring customers
receive faster and more efficient support. Now, 70% of inquiries are resolved
instantly within the chat channel, and support is no longer just a last resort --
it is an extension of the Life360 experience that customers actively engage with.

The implementation created 10x the volume that was expected, but the containment
and automated resolution remains very high. The AI agent handles volume surges
easily, so the agent-level increase was basically flat.

Vida provides 24/7 support and answers very quickly, almost instantly. Handle
times on the AI agent chats are much lower than live agent chats. The deployment
achieved rapid ROI within months, with ongoing cost savings. Response times
became nearly instantaneous, and the AI agent matches human agent performance
while handling five times more volume.

With Vida handling everyday inquiries, human agents now have more bandwidth to
focus on strategic, high-value work, such as complex problem-solving and
personalized customer care.
