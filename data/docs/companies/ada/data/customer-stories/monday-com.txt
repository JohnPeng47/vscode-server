monday.com Case Study | Ada

How monday.com Deploys Ada's AI Agent Across Channels for Impactful Efficiency Gains

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

COMPANY OVERVIEW

monday.com is a work operating system where organizations of any size can create
the tools and processes they need to manage every aspect of their work. By
combining building blocks, like apps, automations, and integrations, teams can
build or customize the workflows of their dreams.

Tal Gulst, Bot Manager at monday.com, integrated a generative AI agent into
their workflow.

"It's conversational, and really understands the customer's issue."
-- Tal Gulst, Bot Manager, monday.com

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE CHALLENGE: SCRIPTED BOTS WERE INFLEXIBLE

The team quickly found scripted bots to be inflexible and time-consuming for
their support team to manage. Their chatbot approach was hard to maintain and
needed constant manual updates.

Before Ada launched the AI agent, monday.com's customer experience team managed
chats, emails, and callbacks globally, with 76% of their conversation volume
coming from the chat channel. They were looking for a dynamic solution to
automatically resolve customer inquiries on chat.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE SOLUTION: ADA'S AI AGENT

Just turning on the AI agent resulted in 40-45% containment. Since onboarding
the AI agent, their containment rate has grown month over month, with
overwhelmingly positive customer feedback. monday.com's highest volume channel
was now proving to be their most successful.

The AI agent's ability to perform actions on behalf of customers was a
game-changer for the monday.com team. Tal connected the AI agent to monday.com's
business systems and taught it how to solve complex customer problems -- just
like a human agent.

The AI agent solves many different complex problems for customers using actions,
including:

- Following up on failed charges
- Generating receipts
- Sending confirmation emails
- Trial extensions ("if someone wants a trial extension, the AI Agent knows how
  to do it")

The AI Agent automatically resolves 50% of customer service tickets, which has
reduced reliance on external support for ticket management and has allowed the
team to tackle more strategic challenges.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

EXPANDING TO EMAIL

When Ada offered monday.com access to an early access program (EAP) for email,
they were excited to hop on board. Tal quickly found the action-oriented and
malleable solution she had been looking for to increase efficiency on the email
channel.

Billing inquiries constitute almost 50% of all email inquiries. Billing is one
of their biggest channels for emails because customers get emails about their
receipts or upgrades and reply directly because they don't see it on the
platform, and it's very intuitive for them to answer there.

The billing use case was one of their biggest wins for the email channel.

The resource savings with the AI agent on email have been substantial, with a
notable reduction in human resources needed to cover their channels.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

KEY METRICS AND RESULTS

- 76% of conversation volume was on chat (before AI agent)
- 40-45% containment rate immediately upon turning on the AI agent
- Containment rate has grown month over month since onboarding
- 50% of customer service tickets automatically resolved by AI agent
- ~50% of all email inquiries are billing-related (now handled by AI)
- Overwhelmingly positive customer feedback

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CROSS-CHANNEL IMPACT

Ada has had a huge impact on operational efficiency. Now, they have the same
"brain" and logic powering both their email and chat channels. If they're
building a flow to improve the customer experience with their chat channel,
their customers reaching out via email can enjoy that same experience.

"Ada is flexible. With Ada, we know the automated resolution will continue to
improve as the AI agent learns and grows. There's significant flexibility in
making changes and improvements. The responses and the accuracy are phenomenal."
-- Tal Gulst, Bot Manager, monday.com
