Title: Ada AI Drives £600K+ Monthly Revenue at Simba

================================================================================

CASE STUDY: SIMBA SLEEP x ADA

================================================================================

OVERVIEW

Simba Sleep grew from a start-up to a £130 million powerhouse in the competitive
sleep technology industry. As a B Corp-certified mattress company, Simba faced
the challenge of keeping customer experience personal and high-quality without
ballooning support team headcount. They partnered with Ada, an AI customer
experience platform, to deploy a generative AI agent and transform their
customer service operations.

Graham Paddon, Customer Experience Director at Simba Sleep, led the
implementation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE CHALLENGE

Simba's 24-person customer service team handles all customer touchpoints,
including web chat, email, and social community management. Customer service
interactions were dominated by routine requests like order updates, delivery
issues, and product questions. Sales conversations were being drowned out by
admin, and scaling support -- especially to 24/7 coverage -- would have required
doubling both cost and headcount.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE SOLUTION

Simba deployed Ada's AI agent, which they lovingly named Luna. Rather than using
Ada's earlier declarative (scripted) chatbot, Simba embraced the generative AI
version.

The transition was straightforward. As described by the Simba team: "It's
basically a couple of clicks: point it to its brain, tell it this is your
knowledge, finesse it with some guidance, and train it as you go."

Simba conducted an A/B test between the declarative and generative versions of
the AI agent. The generative AI agent outperformed in every metric --
containment rate, automated resolution rate, and customer satisfaction.

Luna undergoes weekly QA meetings alongside human agents, complete with
scorecards assessing accuracy, tone, compliance, and customer satisfaction.
Simba celebrates Luna in all dashboards, agent reports, and quality assurance
programs, just like any other staff member.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

KEY METRICS AND RESULTS

- £600,000+ in additional monthly revenue unlocked
- Luna resolves an average of 1,000 conversations per week across channels
- Equivalent workload of 8 full-time agents, without increasing headcount
- 3 human agents redirected from routine support to sales follow-ups
  (abandoned carts and sales callbacks)
- 24/7 customer support coverage achieved without doubling costs or headcount
- Generative AI outperformed declarative AI in every metric: containment rate,
  automated resolution rate, and customer satisfaction

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STRATEGIC APPROACH

Rather than treating AI deflection as a cost-cutting measure, Simba took a
strategic approach to automation. Instead of simply aiming for the highest
possible automated resolution rate, they focused on creating extraordinary
experiences, leveraging the strengths of both automation and personal support to
truly add value for customers and the business.

By automating routine tasks with Ada, Simba gave human agents space to engage on
higher-value topics. By shifting just 3 agents to focus on abandoned carts and
sales callbacks, Simba's customer service team now generates approximately
£600,000 per month in additional revenue. This approach maintained team culture
while protecting workforce stability and driving measurable financial growth.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

MARKET EXPANSION

When moving to new markets, Simba can make simple adjustments to Ada with Luna
and one AI Manager providing full support for the first six months or even the
first year. This dramatically reduces the cost and complexity of international
expansion.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT'S NEXT

Simba is already working on expanding Luna's proactive presence across the
website, enhancing her ability to take action on customer requests, and
continuing to evolve QA and knowledge management practices to keep service sharp.
