================================================================================
Tilt | Ada Case Study
================================================================================

Tilt Transformed CX with Ada, Reaching 84% Automated Resolutions, Faster
Replies, and Smarter AI Support Across Channels

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Key Metrics
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- 84% automated resolution rate
- 64% of email inquiries resolved by AI agent with no human intervention
- Customer satisfaction (CSAT) increased by 8 points on average
- CSAT increased by as much as 15 points in some cases
- 100% of chat volume handled by AI agent

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Challenge
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

As Tilt's customer base grew, so did their support backlog. To scale without
adding headcount, they partnered with Ada.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Email: The First Channel
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Tilt deployed a generative AI agent to handle the rising volume of email
inquiries, delivering faster, more accurate support without increasing strain on
the team. While they expected the AI agent to help, the results surpassed
expectations: 64% of email inquiries were resolved by the AI agent, with no
human intervention required.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Expansion to Chat
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Following the success of the email pilot program, Tilt extended their AI agent
to chat. In chat, customers must be logged in, giving the AI agent access to
richer, account-level context through various APIs. This unlocks the ability to
handle higher-value inquiries and deliver more personalized service.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Greeting API and Personalization
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Tilt's team developed a greeting API that pulls high-level user information at
the start of the conversation, allowing the AI agent to dynamically filter which
playbooks, processes, and knowledge to surface. This makes support more targeted
and preemptive from the very first message.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Coaching and Playbooks
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Performance improvements are attributed to Ada's Coaching and Playbooks
features, which allow the AI agent to become more dynamic and responsive rather
than follow a rigid, predefined flow. Since launching their AI agent, customer
satisfaction has increased by 8 points on average -- and in some cases, by as
much as 15.

Tilt's AI agent has not only matched the accuracy of human agents but is now
exceeding it in key areas.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Impact and Results
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The AI agent now handles routine inquiries with speed and accuracy, freeing up
Tilt's customer support team to focus on high-value interactions, building
customer relationships, and providing insights that will inform important
business decisions.

Ada's AI agent currently handles 100% of Tilt's chat volume.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Future Plans
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Tilt plans to further expand Ada's coverage to their voice channel, continuing
to scale AI-powered support across all customer touchpoints.
