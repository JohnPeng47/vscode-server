================================================================================
Trust & Will Scales Customer Support with Their AI Agent
================================================================================

Company: Trust & Will
Industry: Legal Tech / Estate Planning
AI Agent Name: Will-E
Key Metric: 70% automated resolution rate (achieved in less than a year)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Estate planning is an emotionally charged task where people must make deeply
personal, life-defining decisions. For Trust & Will, providing clarity, empathy,
and accuracy wasn't just important -- it was mission-critical.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE CHALLENGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Trust & Will's initial scripted chatbot offered basic support, but was not able
to handle the nuanced situations that members contact their support team about
every day. Many of their members struggled with the old bot, which had the
classic looping experience and lacked the empathy required for the types of
questions on members' minds.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE SOLUTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Trust & Will partnered with Ada to launch an AI agent they affectionately named
Will-E. Unlike its predecessor, Will-E leverages generative AI and the best
artificial intelligence models to provide scalable, automated resolutions that
combine efficiency with the quality of human-like interactions.

To ensure that generated responses are always safe, accurate, and relevant, the
AI agent is designed to only pull information from company content. This is
especially important in highly regulated industries such as legal tech.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TRAINING AND CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Trust & Will support team treated Will-E like a new team member, dedicating
significant time to its training and continuous improvement to refine responses
and stay aligned with evolving customer needs.

Coaching Will-E is like training a new employee -- but faster and more
consistent. By aligning the AI agent with updated FAQs and real-time insights,
they created a self-improving system that delivers personalized support to
customers at scale.

The team thoroughly audited and revamped their knowledge base, closing content
gaps and collaborating closely with Ada experts to optimize performance. This
iterative process allowed Trust & Will to identify knowledge gaps and make
targeted improvements, ensuring Will-E stayed responsive and effective.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RESULTS AND IMPACT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Will-E's introduction marked a turning point for Trust & Will's service
operations. By covering routine inquiries, Will-E empowered the team to focus
on high-stakes, emotionally charged cases.

In less than a year, Will-E has grown and maintained a steady 70% automated
resolution rate. This has been a major factor in allowing Trust & Will to offer
world-class support from a small but mighty in-house team.

Will-E has been a game-changer, significantly reducing escalations and making a
huge difference in improving the customer experience while lightening the team's
workload.
