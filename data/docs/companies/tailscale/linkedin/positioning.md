## Analysis: The Actual Arc

The claim that Tailscale shifted from "security is broken" to "we save you money" is **directionally correct but the framing is wrong**. It's not a binary pivot. It's a five-phase maturation:

| Phase | Period | Framing | Tone |
|-------|--------|---------|------|
| 1 | Jul 2025 | "Everything is broken" (Zero Trust report) | Fear stats |
| 2 | Aug–Sep 2025 | Community warmth + product delight | Friendly, builder |
| 3 | Oct–Nov 2025 | Platform credibility (awards, features, anti-SASE) | Confident |
| 4 | Jan–Feb 2026 | AI infrastructure play (Aperture) | Visionary |
| 5 | Mar–Apr 2026 | Economic proof (Forrester TEI) | Business case |

They didn't abandon security messaging — they **graduated** from "your VPN sucks" to "here's an auditable, identity-native connectivity platform for AI workloads that also saves you $1.2M."

The Forrester study isn't replacing the zero trust report — it's the logical next step after you've already convinced people the problem exists.
