
COX'S PRIMITIVE:  ISSUER-TRUST LAUNDERING
=========================================

The fraud is not in the documents - every document is genuine.  It is in the
issuance path.  Each issuer in a chain accepts the *output* of an earlier issuer
as proof-of-identity, with no shared view of upstream issuance facts.  Trust
laundered through one weak step becomes admissible everywhere downstream.

THE CHAIN
─────────
conversational PII   (name, DOB, SSN, MMN, POB - the KBA quintuple)
        │
        ▼
vital records / SSA  -> birth cert + SSN card        (mail, no in-person check)
        │
        ▼
state DMV           -> driver's license              (the keystone credential)
        │
        ├───> banks, auto loans, secured credit, notary commission
        ├───> US Department of State -> passport
        ├───> 2nd state's DMV -> 2nd DL (uses 1st DL as primary ID)
        └───> court -> legal name change (resets the paper trail)

WHY IT COMPOUNDS
────────────────
- each new credential is admissible as primary ID at the next issuer
- no issuer queries any other issuer's gallery (DMV, DoS, SSA all siloed)
- the credential's authenticity (hologram, seal, MRZ) is genuine because the
issuance was genuine - the falseness is in the binding to a person

THE 7-DMV STACK  (the specific attack Cox ran against state DMVs)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Every state's DMV accepts another state's DL as primary ID for issuing a new
DL, and in the early-2000s no DMV ran 1:N facial matching across either its
own gallery or any other state's gallery.  So one keystone DL bootstraps a
chain of fully legitimate licenses, all in the same name and on the same face:

      AL DMV  ─[breeder docs]──>   Alabama DL    (Cox's face, identity #1)
                                        │
      GA DMV  ─[Alabama DL]────>   Georgia DL    (same face, same name)
                                        │
      FL DMV  ─[Georgia DL]────>   Florida DL    (same face, same name)
                                        │
                                       ...        ... 7 states deep

Then repeat the whole stack under a different seed (Track A or Track B) and
the same face is now bound to N parallel identities, each holding several
state DLs.  Final scoreboard: 27 DLs across 7 states - one face on every one
of them, and not a single forged document in the pile.

The one signal that breaks this is 1:N face matching across DMV galleries.
AAMVA's State-to-State program does exactly that today; the "Photo List"
idea referenced in section 2 below is the same primitive applied to Persona's
own gallery of inquiry selfies - any new inquiry whose selfie matches a face
already issued under a different identity is the Cox-pattern fingerprint.

TWO WAYS TO SEED THE CHAIN
──────────────────────────
Identity-theft seed             Synthetic seed
(Track A, "homeless survey")    (Track B, child SSN or SSA-issued SSN)
- real adult, dormant           - no real adult exists
- residual collision risk       - ~zero collision risk
- real birth cert               - fabricated birth cert (custom seal)

WHY IT WORKED THEN, WHY IT WOULD STRUGGLE NOW
─────────────────────────────────────────────
paper-trust issuance + siloed verification = the loop closes only at the face,
and nothing in 2003 looked at the face across issuers.  Modern mitigations
(DMV 1:N facial dedup, AAMVA S2S, DoS passport gallery, mDL cryptographic
signing, IAL2 selfie+liveness) all attack this exact gap.  The face becomes
the cross-issuer anchor the documents never were.

TOTALS THIS PRIMITIVE PRODUCED FOR COX:  50+ identities · 27 DLs / 7 states · ~24 passports.
